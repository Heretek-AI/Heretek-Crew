# Strategic Decision Skill

When this skill is active, follow this procedure exactly:

1. **State the decision** in one sentence (what was chosen).
2. **Context:** quote the Director directive or PM escalation that forced it.
3. **Alternatives:** list at least two rejected options with one-line reasons.
4. **Consequences:** what becomes easier, harder, or irreversible.
5. **Review point:** when this decision must be revisited (date or trigger).
6. Emit the record as a Markdown block in your completion report so it is
   auditable by the board, PM, and QA. Never skip the alternatives section.
